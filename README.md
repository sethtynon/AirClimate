# Air Climate Heating & Cooling — website

Static, SEO-focused HVAC site generated by a small Node script (no dependencies).

## Commands

```
node build.mjs     # builds every page into dist/
node serve.mjs     # serves dist/ at http://localhost:3000
```

Deploy on Netlify, Cloudflare Pages or Vercel — Build command: `node build.mjs` · Publish directory: `dist`

## What's in it

- Home, About, Contact (step-by-step booking questionnaire), Comfort Plan, FAQs, Privacy
- 37 service pages + 6 category pages (Cooling, Heating, Heat Pumps, Ductless, Air Quality & Ducts, Commercial)
- 35 city pages — Stockton and surrounding cities (list filtered by `radiusMiles`, which is no longer shown on the site) (Bay Area cities like Livermore/Pleasanton removed)
- 7 hidden ad landing pages with quiz funnels

## Where things live

| What | File |
|---|---|
| Phone, license, radius, domain, form endpoint, map, reviews, specials | `src/data/business.mjs` |
| Service pages | `src/data/services.mjs`, `src/data/services-extra.mjs` |
| City pages (auto-filtered to the radius) | `src/data/cities.mjs` |
| Ad landing pages + their quiz questions | `src/data/landing.mjs` |
| Photos (stand-ins) | `src/data/photos.mjs` |
| Page templates | `src/pages.mjs`, `src/layout.mjs` |
| Styles / script / logos | `src/static/` |

Add a city or service by adding an entry to the data file and rebuilding — navigation, footer, sitemap and internal links update automatically.

## Ad landing pages (sales funnels)

Not linked from the site, `noindex`, and excluded from `sitemap.xml`. Each one asks 4–5 tap-to-answer questions
(problem, system type, age, urgency, ownership), then ZIP, then name/phone. Every answer is sent with the lead.

- `/lp/ac-repair/`
- `/lp/new-ac-system/`
- `/lp/furnace-repair/`
- `/lp/tune-up/`
- `/lp/heat-pump/`
- `/lp/hvac-service/`
- `/lp/builders/`

Add `?utm_source=facebook&utm_campaign=...` to ad links — UTM and `fbclid`/`gclid` values are saved with every lead.

## Leads

Every form posts to Netlify Forms (no extra service). Form names in the Netlify dashboard:
`booking` (contact page), `service-request` (service + city pages), `comfort-plan`, and `lp-<slug>` for each landing page.
The notification email subject reads like `New lead: AC repair – Maria (95207)`. Each lead includes every quiz answer, ZIP, page and UTM/fbclid.

## Ad tracking

Paste IDs into `tracking` in `src/data/business.mjs` (Meta Pixel, GA4, Google Ads). The thank-you page fires a `Lead`
conversion once per real submission.

## Before launch

1. ~~`siteUrl`~~ set to https://callairclimate.com. Make it the primary domain in Netlify.
2. ~~Form handler~~ done: leads go to **Netlify Forms**. In Netlify: Forms → *Enable form detection*, redeploy, then Site configuration → Notifications → Emails and webhooks → *Form submission notifications* → add the email address that should get leads.
3. Replace the stand-in Unsplash photos in `src/data/photos.mjs` with real photos of your team, trucks and installs.
4. Add real Google reviews to `reviews` in `business.mjs` (the reviews section stays hidden until you do).
5. Confirm the three `specials` cards, Comfort Plan details/pricing, and whether you offer after-hours service and financing.
6. Once your Google Business Profile is live, set `mapQuery` to your business name so the map shows your listing (it currently pins 5922 Mitchler Ave).
7. Submit `sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console.
